I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group and am supervised by Professor Junxian He. I work at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, on making large language models and vision-language models both more capable and more trustworthy.

About
======
My current research is carried out in the HKUST NLP Group, led by Professor Junxian He, whom I had previously worked with during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was also awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. I joined Shanghai AI Lab as a research intern in the summer of 2023, advised by Prof. Yu Cheng, then moved to Tencent WXG in 2024, advised by Zifei Shan, before starting my PhD at HKUST. I am currently a research intern at MINIMAX.
